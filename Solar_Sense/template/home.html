🌿 Hierarchical Clustering & Dendrogram Analysis

Agglomerative Ward Linkage

Bottom-up agglomerative clustering using Ward's minimum variance linkage criterion. The dendrogram shows how individual data points merge into clusters at different distance thresholds, revealing 3 major operational regimes in the solar generation data.

📊 Cluster Quality Metrics

Silhouette Score measures cluster cohesion vs separation (-1 to 1, higher is better). Calinski-Harabasz is the ratio of between-cluster to within-cluster dispersion. Davies-Bouldin measures cluster similarity (lower is better).
