📐 Understanding Cluster Quality Metrics

Metric Interpretation
📏

Silhouette Score

Measures how similar a point is to its own cluster vs. neighboring clusters. Range: [-1, 1]. Higher is better. A score of 0.58 indicates well-separated, cohesive clusters.

0.584 / 1.0
📐

Calinski-Harabasz Index

Ratio of between-cluster dispersion to within-cluster dispersion. Higher values indicate denser, more separated clusters. Score of 116,290 shows excellent separation.

116,290 — Excellent
🎯

Davies-Bouldin Index

Average similarity between clusters. Lower is better (0 = perfect). A score of 0.62 confirms minimal cluster overlap and distinct operational regimes.

0.6196 — Low Overlap
🔻

Inertia (WCSS)

Within-Cluster Sum of Squares — total distance of points from their cluster centroid. The Elbow method identifies the optimal k where inertia reduction rate flattens.

62,779 — Elbow at k=3

💡 Cluster Interpretation Guide

Domain Analysis

Cluster 0 — Nighttime / Dormant Standby

Zero or near-zero DC power output with low irradiation and ambient module temperatures. Represents nighttime hours and heavy overcast conditions where solar panels are dormant.

DC_POWER ≈ 0 Irradiation < 0.05 Night / Low Light

Cluster 1 — Transitional / Variable Clouds

Moderate DC power output during sunrise/sunset transitions or partially clouded periods. Module temperatures are warm but not at peak, irradiation is moderate.

DC_POWER 0.15–0.55 Dawn / Dusk Partially Overcast

Cluster 2 — Peak Solar Generation

Maximum DC power output during clear-sky midday conditions with high irradiation and elevated module temperatures. This is the optimal operational state for energy harvesting.

DC_POWER > 0.55 Clear Sky Peak Radiance
