About Solar Sense

Pioneering clean energy intelligence through machine learning, telemetry analysis, and automated feature engineering.

☀️ Project Mission & Overview

Solar Sense is an intelligent analytics platform designed to analyze industrial solar power generation data, detect environmental correlation patterns, and forecast clean energy production with mathematical precision.

By bridging meteorological telemetry with advanced machine learning algorithms, the platform empowers plant operators, energy engineers, and grid managers to optimize clean power dispatch and maintain grid stability.

Core Objectives

System Deliverables
  • Environmental Telemetry Analysis: Measure and model real-time solar irradiation, ambient air temperature, and module surface thermals.
  • Pattern Recognition: Discover diurnal cyclical patterns and power degradation signatures across solar PV inverters.
  • Predictive Power Output: Calibrate regression models to forecast DC Power output from input ambient parameters.
  • Feature Engineering: Automate statistical imputation, categorical encodings, and numerical scaling across raw CSV data streams.
  • Renewable Optimization: Provide actionable operational reports supporting net-zero energy integration.
